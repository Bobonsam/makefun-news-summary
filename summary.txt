Indonesia's Vice-President Gibran resigned after courts ruled his schooling ineligible, and MPR chair Muzani declared the post vacant; Prabowo responded, the MPR elects a successor in 60 days from two nominees named in 14 days, and the rupiah hit a record low Rp18,240 per dollar. US-mediated Russia-Ukraine talks in Abu Dhabi agreed a prisoner swap, no territorial breakthrough. Iran's Rezaei said Hormuz stays shut until the US meets seven conditions; Guards claimed three tankers hit mines, unverified, and the WSJ said Vice President Vance weighs direct talks with Rezaei. COP31's summit opened in Antalya; Guterres warned delay puts the world on a path to destruction. US October CPI rose 0.4%, above 0.3% expected, core up 0.3%, the BLS said, lifting December Fed-hike bets. Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang resigned after admitting affairs, one with a suspected Chinese spy; CFO Colette Kress became interim CEO; shares had slid about 8% after hours on reports the board would oust him. Democrat Colom beat Hyde-Smith in Mississippi, the AP called, the state's first Black senator since Reconstruction, giving Democrats 55 seats. Congo's Ebola outbreak hit 11,042 cases and 5,387 deaths, the second-largest on record. The House voted 211-204 to refer eight impeachment articles against Defense Secretary Hegseth to committee, blocking a floor vote. Israel swore in a new Knesset; Eisenkot was asked to form a government after Netanyahu's bloc won 53 seats, short of a majority. SoftBank posted a 1.115 trillion yen loss. US prosecutors charged three Iran-backed operatives over alleged plots including RAF Fairford. A judge let Trump's $10bn BBC defamation suit over a Panorama edit proceed, dismissing a trade-practices claim, Reuters said. An 86-vehicle fog pile-up on Bulgaria's Trakia motorway killed 12 and injured 46, AP said; a day of mourning was declared. Storm Charles hit Portugal, Reuters said, cancelling 200-plus flights, shutting schools.
